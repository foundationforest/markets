# Directory

Every market in [`directory.json`](directory.json), one line each. [`build/check.sh`](build/check.sh)
writes this file from it. What a market is: [`README.md`](README.md).

- [`car-rental`](markets/car-rental.json): Owners let someone use their car for a day or a week, for money. Roles: owner (offers), renter (requests).
- [`electronics`](markets/electronics.json): People sell phones, laptops, consoles and other electronics they own, picked up in person or shipped. Roles: seller (offers), buyer (requests).
- [`home-cleaning`](markets/home-cleaning.json): Cleaners clean people's homes, by the hour or per visit, often every week. Roles: cleaner (offers), client (requests).
- [`sports-coaching`](markets/sports-coaching.json): Coaches teach a sport one on one or in small groups, paid per session, in person or online. Roles: coach (offers), student (requests).
- [`tennis-matches`](markets/tennis-matches.json): Tennis players find someone to play a match with, for no money. Roles: player (posts).
- [`tutoring`](markets/tutoring.json): Tutors help students with school subjects one on one, paid per hour, in person or online. Roles: tutor (offers), student (requests).
