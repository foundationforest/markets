# Directory

The markets the foundation recommends, by folder: what each is, its sides, and whether money moves.
How markets work and how to add one: [`README.md`](README.md).

## home

Work done at the buyer's home or garden.

- [`plumbing`](home/plumbing.json): Pipes, taps, drains, toilets and water heaters: fixing and fitting. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`electrical`](home/electrical.json): Wiring, sockets, lights and fuse boxes: fixing and fitting. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`cleaning`](home/cleaning.json): Cleaning a home, once or on a schedule. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`gardening`](home/gardening.json): Mowing, pruning, planting and clearing a garden or yard. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`handyman`](home/handyman.json): Small repairs and jobs around a home: shelves, doors, putting furniture together. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`moving`](home/moving.json): Moving a home's things from one place to another, with or without packing. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`painting`](home/painting.json): Painting and decorating walls, ceilings, doors and outside walls. Two-sided. Money moves.

## vehicles

Vehicles bought, sold and rented.

- [`cars`](vehicles/cars.json): Cars, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`motorcycles`](vehicles/motorcycles.json): Motorcycles and scooters, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`car-rental`](vehicles/car-rental.json): Cars rented from their owner or a rental business. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`bikes`](vehicles/bikes.json): Bicycles and electric bikes, and their parts. Two-sided. Money moves.

## property

Homes and spaces sold, rented, stayed in or booked.

- [`property-sales`](property/property-sales.json): Homes, flats and land, sold. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`property-rentals`](property/property-rentals.json): Homes and rooms rented to live in. Two-sided: landlord and tenant. Money moves.
- [`stays`](property/stays.json): Rooms, flats and houses to stay in while away. Two-sided: host and guest. Money moves.
- [`venues`](property/venues.json): Halls, rooms and spaces booked for events and meetings. Two-sided. Money moves.

## electronics

Devices and machines that run on power, new or used.

- [`electronics`](electronics/electronics.json): Phones, computers, tablets, cameras, consoles and their parts, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`appliances`](electronics/appliances.json): Fridges, washing machines, ovens and small home appliances, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.

## fashion

Things to wear, new or used.

- [`clothing`](fashion/clothing.json): Clothes, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`shoes`](fashion/shoes.json): Shoes and boots, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`accessories`](fashion/accessories.json): Bags, watches, jewellery, belts, hats and sunglasses, new or used. Two-sided. Money moves.

## education

Learning from someone who teaches.

- [`tutoring`](education/tutoring.json): Lessons in any subject, online or in person. Two-sided: teacher and student. Money moves.
- [`courses`](education/courses.json): Courses taught over several sessions, online or in person, to one person or many. Two-sided: teacher and student. Money moves.
- [`music-lessons`](education/music-lessons.json): Lessons in playing an instrument or singing, online or in person. Two-sided: teacher and student. Money moves.

## health-and-beauty

Care for how people look and feel, given by a person.

- [`hair`](health-and-beauty/hair.json): Haircuts, colour and styling, at a salon or at home. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`massage`](health-and-beauty/massage.json): Massage, at the seller's place or at home. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`personal-training`](health-and-beauty/personal-training.json): Training sessions with a coach, in a gym, outdoors, at home or online. Two-sided. Money moves.

## food

Food cooked by one person for another.

- [`private-chef`](food/private-chef.json): A cook who shops and cooks a meal at the buyer's place. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`catering`](food/catering.json): Food cooked and served for events, from small parties to weddings. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`meals`](food/meals.json): Home-cooked meals, picked up or delivered. Two-sided. Money moves.

## events

Getting into events, and the people who work them.

- [`tickets`](events/tickets.json): Tickets to concerts, games, shows and other events, resold. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`experiences`](events/experiences.json): Tours, classes, tastings and other activities led by a person. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`event-staff`](events/event-staff.json): Waiters, bar staff, hosts and helpers for events. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`security`](events/security.json): Guards and door staff for events and places. Two-sided. Money moves.

## travel-and-stays

Trips and places to stay while away.

No markets yet.

## transport

People and things moved from one place to another.

- [`rides`](transport/rides.json): Rides in someone's car, across town or between cities. Two-sided: driver and rider. Money moves.
- [`deliveries`](transport/deliveries.json): Parcels, food and errands carried across town. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`freight`](transport/freight.json): Large loads, pallets and vehicles carried long distances. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`chauffeur`](transport/chauffeur.json): A driver for hours or days, in the driver's car or the buyer's. Two-sided. Money moves.

## business

Professional services for people and companies.

- [`consulting`](business/consulting.json): Advice and projects for a business, from someone who knows the field. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`accounting`](business/accounting.json): Bookkeeping, tax returns and accounts. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`legal`](business/legal.json): Legal advice, documents and representation. Two-sided. Money moves.

## creative-and-digital

Work made by hand or on a computer, and delivered.

- [`design`](creative-and-digital/design.json): Logos, graphics, layouts and other visual design, delivered as files. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`video-editing`](creative-and-digital/video-editing.json): Cutting and finishing video from the buyer's footage. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`coding`](creative-and-digital/coding.json): Code written to order: apps, sites, scripts and fixes. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`writing`](creative-and-digital/writing.json): Articles, copy and other text, written to order. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`translation`](creative-and-digital/translation.json): Text translated from one language to another. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`photography`](creative-and-digital/photography.json): Photo shoots of people, events, products and places, delivered as edited photos. Two-sided. Money moves.

## media

Things made to look at, watch or read, free or paid.

- [`photos`](media/photos.json): Photos to view or license, from the person who took them. Two-sided: creator and viewer. Money moves.
- [`videos`](media/videos.json): Videos, from the person who made them. Two-sided: creator and viewer. Money moves.
- [`films`](media/films.json): Films and documentaries, from the people who made them. Two-sided: creator and viewer. Money moves.
- [`short-form`](media/short-form.json): Short clips made to watch in one go on a phone. Two-sided: creator and viewer. Money moves.
- [`news`](media/news.json): Reporting and analysis, from the person who wrote or filmed it. Two-sided: creator and viewer. Money moves.

## pets

Care for animals, from someone other than their owner.

- [`pet-sitting`](pets/pet-sitting.json): Looking after pets at their home or the sitter's, and dog walking. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`grooming`](pets/grooming.json): Washing, trimming and nail care for pets, at a salon or at home. Two-sided. Money moves.
- [`training`](pets/training.json): Teaching pets to behave, one to one or in classes. Two-sided. Money moves.

## sports

Finding people to play or train with.

- [`tennis`](sports/tennis.json): Finding someone to play tennis with. One-sided. No money.
- [`running-partners`](sports/running-partners.json): Finding someone to run with. One-sided. No money.

## community

People meeting to learn or do things together.

- [`study-partners`](community/study-partners.json): Finding someone to study with, in person or online. One-sided. No money.
