# Directory

Every market in [`markets/`](markets/), one line each. [`build/check.sh`](build/check.sh) writes
this file and [`directory.json`](directory.json) from the market files; nobody edits either by
hand. What a market is: [`README.md`](README.md).

A live market needs no payment between people yet, is polished, and from now on only grows. A
beta market works, at your own risk, and may still change in any way.

- [`freelance-design`](markets/freelance-design.json) (beta): Freelancers design logos, brands, websites, apps and illustrations, delivered remotely; pay piece by piece, each piece once delivered, so neither side risks much. Roles: freelancer (offers), client (requests).
- [`freelance-software`](markets/freelance-software.json) (beta): Freelancers build and fix software, delivered remotely; pay piece by piece, each piece once delivered, so neither side risks much. Roles: freelancer (offers), client (requests).
- [`freelance-video`](markets/freelance-video.json) (beta): Freelancers edit videos and make animations and motion graphics, delivered remotely, not filming at events; pay piece by piece, each piece once delivered, so neither side risks much. Roles: freelancer (offers), client (requests).
- [`goods`](markets/goods.json) (beta): People sell everyday things, new or used, handed over and paid in person, or shipped when the price is one either side could lose; not motor vehicles or homes. Roles: seller (offers), buyer (requests).
- [`home-cleaning`](markets/home-cleaning.json) (beta): Cleaners clean people's homes, paid by the visit, often every week. Roles: cleaner (offers), client (requests).
- [`home-repair`](markets/home-repair.json) (beta): Repairers do plumbing, electrical and handyman jobs in people's homes, each job paid once it is done; not renovations paid in stages. Roles: repairer (offers), client (requests).
- [`language-exchange`](markets/language-exchange.json) (beta): Two people practise each other's language, taking turns, online or in person, with no money between them. For adults. Roles: partner (requests).
- [`sports-coaching`](markets/sports-coaching.json) (beta): Coaches teach a sport or fitness, one on one or in groups, in person or online, paid by the session or the hour; the student is whoever arranges the coaching, often a parent, who stays present for a child's session in person. Roles: coach (offers), student (requests).
- [`stays-free`](markets/stays-free.json) (beta): Hosts give guests a couch or a room for free, asking only for good company: no tasks and no money between them. For adults. Roles: host (offers), guest (requests).
- [`stays-work`](markets/stays-work.json) (beta): Hosts give guests a bed, and often meals, for their help, such as on a farm or in a hostel, building, or sitting a house or pets, with no money between them. For adults. Roles: host (offers), guest (requests).
- [`tours`](markets/tours.json) (beta): Local guides show a group around, such as on a food, walking, history or nature tour, paid on the day. For adults. Roles: guide (offers), guest (requests).
- [`tutoring`](markets/tutoring.json) (beta): Tutors teach school subjects, languages, chess and other desk subjects, online or in person, paid by the lesson or the hour; the student is whoever arranges the lessons, often a parent, who stays present for a child's lesson in person. Roles: tutor (offers), student (requests).
